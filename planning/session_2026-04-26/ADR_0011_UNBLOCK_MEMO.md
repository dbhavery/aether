# ADR-0011 Unblock Memo

**Date:** 2026-04-28
**Source:** `planning/session_2026-04-26/REAL_CORPUS_BENCH_FINDINGS.md`

The prior deferral reason ("wait for real conversation data") is **no longer the operative blocker** — but ADR-0011 is still **not unblockable**, for a different reason that the bench surfaced. The Lex Fridman bench (417 segments, bge-m3 via Ollama) achieved 28.8–33.3% recall@5 against a structural-lexical-reuse ground truth, which is 18–28× better than random and confirms bge-m3 is doing real work. However, miss-pattern inspection shows that whenever bge-m3 misses, it does so by retrieving turns that are *more* semantically relevant than the lexical-reuse "ground truth" turn — i.e., automatic ground truth on real transcripts systematically penalizes good semantic retrieval, while the synthetic-corpus +2.8pp cross-encoder lift is partly an artifact of synthetic ground truth being constructed to match a single crisp target. Either method gives an adversarial yardstick for measuring rerank lift, so retrieval-recall benchmarks — synthetic *or* real-conversation — cannot inform whether ADR-0011 should ship. The actual blocker is now **downstream-task quality signal from real Aether usage** (correct fact recall on later turns, multi-turn synthesis correctness, post-retrieval correction-rate via the existing `tier:changed` bus), none of which require (query, target) ground-truth pairs but all of which require Aether to be in real conversational use.

**Verdict: STILL-DEFER.** Specific gap: no downstream-task signal source exists yet, and pure retrieval-recall is methodologically unable to fill the gap. Recommended ADR-0011 status field: `Deferred — blocked on downstream-task signal, not on bench data` (replacing the older "wait for real conversation data" language, which the bench has now invalidated as a sufficient unblock condition).
