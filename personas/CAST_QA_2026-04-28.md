## Method

For each of the 9 personas, the creator opened `avatar/anchor.png`
in the chat (rendered inline) and assessed:
1. Single-subject composition (no double-faces, no ghosted figures)
2. Anchor schema compliance (even diffuse light, simple background,
   neutral expression with mouth softly closed, head + shoulders
   composition, real skin texture)
3. Cross-pack distinctiveness (no two anchors visually confusable
   from a thumbnail)

Then scanned `portrait.png`, `profile_left.png`, `profile_right.png`
for face-consistency with the anchor (same person, same hair color,
same eye color, same approximate face geometry).

  inline anti-doubling suffix; v2 clean. v1 raw preserved at

  `…anchor_raw_v2.jpg`.

> "Single subject only, exactly one person in the entire frame, no
> second figure, no mirror, no reflection, no ghosted face, no double

the standard anchor prompt template inside each persona's
