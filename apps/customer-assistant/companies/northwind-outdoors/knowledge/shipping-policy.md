# Shipping Policy

_Sample policy for the fictional company "Northwind Outdoors". For demo use only._

## Shipping options and costs

Northwind Outdoors ships to all 50 US states and to Canada.

| Method | Cost | Delivery estimate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Ground | Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95 | 3–7 business days |
| Expedited | $14.95 | 2 business days |
| Overnight | $29.95 | Next business day if ordered before 12pm PT |
| Canada Standard | $19.95 | 7–12 business days |

Orders placed before 12:00pm Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day.
Orders placed after that, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business
day.

## Order processing

Most in-stock orders are processed within one business day. During major sales
(for example, our Trailhead Sale each spring) processing can take up to three
business days. You'll get a shipping confirmation email with tracking as soon
as your order leaves our warehouse in Bend, Oregon.

## Tracking your order

Every shipment includes a tracking number sent to the email on your order. You
can also see tracking under **Account → Orders**. If your tracking hasn't
updated in more than three business days, contact support so we can open a
trace with the carrier.

## International shipping

We currently ship internationally only to Canada. Duties and import taxes for
Canadian orders are the responsibility of the recipient and are collected by
the carrier at delivery. We do not ship to PO boxes outside the US.

## Lost or delayed packages

If a tracking number shows "delivered" but you can't find the package, check
with neighbors and your building's mail room first, then contact us within 7
days. For packages that stop updating in transit for more than 7 business days,
we'll file a carrier claim and send a replacement once the claim is confirmed.
