# Product FAQ

_Sample FAQ for the fictional company "Northwind Outdoors". For demo use only._

## Sizing

**How do your jackets fit?** Our shell jackets fit true to size with room for a
midlayer. If you're between sizes and plan to layer heavily, size up. Every
product page has a size chart with chest, waist, and sleeve measurements.

**Do your boots run large or small?** Our hiking boots run about a half size
large. If you're a true 10, most customers are happiest in our 9.5. We
recommend trying them on with the socks you'll actually hike in.

## Materials and care

**Are your rain shells actually waterproof?** Yes. Our shells use a 3-layer
waterproof-breathable membrane rated to a 20,000mm water column. Waterproofing
relies on the factory DWR finish; reapply a spray-on DWR treatment once water
stops beading.

**How do I wash a waterproof jacket?** Use a technical wash (not regular
detergent), run a gentle cycle, and tumble dry low to reactivate the DWR. Skip
fabric softener — it clogs the membrane.

**How do I care for a down sleeping bag?** Store it uncompressed in the cotton
sack it ships with. Wash rarely, with down-specific soap, and dry on low with
clean tennis balls to break up clumps.

## Products and stock

**Do you restock sold-out items?** Popular items usually restock within 2–4
weeks. Use the "Notify me" button on a product page to get an email when your
size is back.

**Are your products made sustainably?** Our flagship shells use 100% recycled
face fabric and we're bluesign-approved. Full sustainability details are on
each product page.

## Gift cards

**Do gift cards expire?** No. Northwind gift cards never expire and have no
fees. They can be used online or applied to a phone order with support.
