# Returns and Exchanges

_Sample policy for the fictional company "Northwind Outdoors". For demo use only._

## Return window

You can return most items within **60 days of delivery** for a full refund to
your original payment method. Items must be unused, with original tags
attached, and in their original packaging. Worn or washed apparel and used gear
cannot be returned unless covered by warranty.

## How to start a return

1. Go to **Account → Orders** and select the order.
2. Choose **Start a Return** and pick the items and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label we email you.
4. Drop the package at any carrier location within 14 days of creating the
   return.

Returns are free for orders shipped within the US. For Canadian orders, the
customer pays return shipping.

## Refund timing

Once our warehouse receives and inspects your return (usually 2–3 business
days), we issue the refund. Refunds post to your original payment method within
5–7 business days after that. We'll email you when the refund is issued.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange for a different size or color is to return the
original item for a refund and place a new order. If the item you want is in
stock, we can also process a direct size exchange with free reshipping — just
ask support.

## Non-returnable items

- Gift cards
- Final-sale and clearance items marked "no returns"
- Used climbing safety gear (harnesses, ropes, carabiners) for safety reasons
- Custom-engraved products

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or is defective, don't start a standard return —
contact support within 30 days so we can replace it at no cost and cover return
shipping. Defects after 30 days may be covered under our warranty (see
warranty.md).
