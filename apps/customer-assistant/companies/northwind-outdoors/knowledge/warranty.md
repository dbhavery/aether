# Warranty Coverage

_Sample policy for the fictional company "Northwind Outdoors". For demo use only._

## The Northwind Promise

Every product we make is covered by the **Northwind Promise**: a limited
warranty against defects in materials and workmanship for the practical
lifetime of the product. This covers things like seams that fail, zippers that
break under normal use, delaminating waterproof membranes, and hardware that
cracks or bends without misuse.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects (stitching, bonding, hardware failure)
- Waterproof membrane failure under normal use
- Zipper and buckle failure not caused by abuse

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear (worn-out soles, faded fabric, general aging)
- Damage from accidents, improper use, or improper care
- Damage from alterations or repairs not done by Northwind
- Lost or stolen items
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect function

## How to file a warranty claim

1. Email **warranty@northwind-outdoors.example.com** or use **Account →
   Warranty Claim**.
2. Include your order number (or proof of purchase), the product name, and
   clear photos of the issue.
3. Our gear team reviews most claims within 5 business days.

If we approve the claim, we'll repair the item, replace it, or issue store
credit — our choice based on what's available. You cover shipping to us; we
cover shipping the repaired or replacement item back to you.

## Lifetime definition

"Practical lifetime" means the normal usable life of the product, not the
lifetime of the owner. A backpack expected to last ten years of regular use is
not defective simply because it wears out after a decade of heavy trips.
