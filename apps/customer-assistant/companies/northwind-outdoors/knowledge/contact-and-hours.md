## How to reach us

- **Email:** support@northwind-outdoors.example.com (replies within 1 business day)
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-0142
- **Live chat:** available on our website during support hours
- **Warranty claims:** warranty@northwind-outdoors.example.com

## Support hours

Our support team is available:

- **Monday–Friday:** 8:00am – 6:00pm Pacific Time
- **Saturday:** 9:00am – 2:00pm Pacific Time
- **Sunday and major US holidays:** closed

Outside these hours you can still browse help articles and start a return or
warranty claim online; a team member will follow up the next business day.

## Speaking to a human

If you'd rather talk to a person, ask the assistant to connect you with an
agent, call us during support hours, or reply "agent" in chat. For anything
involving a lost package, a damaged shipment, a refund over $300, or a legal or
chargeback matter, we'll route you to a human specialist right away.
