# Account and Orders

_Sample help article for the fictional company "Northwind Outdoors". For demo use only._

## Managing your account

**Resetting your password.** On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**
and enter your email. We'll send a reset link that's valid for 60 minutes. If
it doesn't arrive, check spam, then contact support.

**Updating your email or address.** Go to **Account → Profile** to change your
email, default shipping address, and communication preferences. Changing your
account address does not change the address on orders that have already
shipped.

## Changing or canceling an order

Orders can be changed or canceled only before they ship. Because in-stock
orders often ship within an hour, contact support as soon as possible. Once an
order has a tracking number, it can't be canceled — but you can refuse delivery
or return it under our returns policy.

**Adding an item to an existing order.** We can't add items to an order once
it's placed. Place a second order; if both ship the same day we'll refund the
extra shipping on request.

## Payment

**Accepted payment methods.** We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express,
Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Northwind gift cards. We do not accept
checks, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency.

**When am I charged?** Your card is authorized at checkout and charged when the
order ships. For pre-orders, you're charged when the item ships, not when you
place the order.

**Promo codes.** Enter promo codes in the cart before checkout. Only one promo
code can be applied per order, and codes can't be combined with final-sale
pricing.

## Order issues

**I received the wrong item.** Contact support within 30 days with your order
number and a photo. We'll send the correct item and a prepaid label for the
wrong one at no cost.

**My order is missing an item.** Sometimes items in one order ship separately.
Check your shipping emails for multiple tracking numbers. If an item is still
missing after all packages arrive, contact support.
